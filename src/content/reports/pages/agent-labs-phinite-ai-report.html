Overview

The Agent Labs build session, facilitated by Phinite AI, brought together over 150 developers, students, and enterprise innovators at ATLAS SkillTech University to focus on the infrastructure challenges of deploying autonomous agents at scale.

Moving beyond simple sandboxes, the event provided a hands-on environment for participants to design, deploy, and observe multi-agent systems using the Phinite platform. The session helped builders move from theoretical AI interest to production-ready, enterprise-grade agentic solutions.
