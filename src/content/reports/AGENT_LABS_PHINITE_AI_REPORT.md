# Agent Labs by Phinite AI Report

Infrastructure for the Agent Economy

Event date: June 28, 2026

## Overview

The Agent Labs build session, facilitated by Phinite AI, brought together over 150 developers, students, and enterprise innovators at ATLAS SkillTech University (BKC) to focus on the infrastructure challenges of deploying autonomous agents at scale.

Moving beyond simple sandboxes, the event provided a hands-on environment for participants to design, deploy, and observe multi-agent systems using the Phinite platform. By hosting this intensive build session, ATLAS SkillTech University, ISDI, reinforced its position as a technical innovation hub and offered the infrastructure needed for builders to move from theoretical AI interest to production-ready, enterprise-grade agentic solutions.

## Key Learnings and Strategic Insights

### 1. Architectural Foundations for Multi-Agent Systems

Speakers: Shashank Somal and Ashish Deekonda

- Production gap: Most AI agent projects fail because teams repeatedly rebuild foundational infrastructure, including identity, skill sets, lifecycle management, and governance.
- Unified infrastructure: The Phinite platform provides a centralized stack to design, deploy, observe, and scale multi-agentic systems, shifting developer focus from infrastructure maintenance to high-value agent logic.

### 2. Live Agent Registry and A2A Communication

- Agent identity and interoperability: Participants engaged in a live walkthrough of the Agent Registry and Agent-to-Agent communication protocols, showing how independent agents interact within a secure, governed framework.
- Lifecycle management: The hands-on build blocks taught attendees how to map the full lifecycle of an agent, from initial registry and skill attachment to active production deployment and observation.
- Curricular adaptation: These sessions provide a blueprint for ATLAS ISDI's future labs, where students can work with platform-native tools to handle complex multi-agent interactions.

### 3. Enterprise-Relevant Solution Building

- Practical applicability: Participants built agentic solutions relevant to current enterprise demands, including L1/L2 customer support agents, product specification writers, and automated legal advisory systems.
- Ease of deployment: The event demonstrated that when developers are provided with the right abstraction layers, they can rapidly prototype and deploy agents that solve complex business logic with minimal friction.

### 4. Scaling Agentic Innovation

- Developer energy and adoption: With over 150 participants building on the Phinite platform, the session highlighted a community shift toward professionalizing the agent development process.
- Frictionless innovation: By offering free accounts, credits, and expert guidance, the build session removed technical barriers and helped participants move from idea to production-ready agent in half a day.
- Ecosystem hub positioning: ATLAS ISDI continues to serve as a physical convergence zone for Mumbai's AI community, giving global platform teams like Phinite AI a direct way to engage the next generation of builders.

## Speakers and Community Leaders

- Shashank Somal: Founder and CEO of Phinite AI, who opened the session with a keynote on the need for robust infrastructure for the agent economy.
- Ashish Deekonda: Head of Product at Phinite AI, who led the technical deep dive into the platform stack and supervised the hands-on build blocks.

## Conclusion

The Agent Labs event at ATLAS SkillTech University demonstrated that the barrier to building the Agent Economy is quickly dissolving as developers gain access to production-grade infrastructure. By providing the physical and technical space for this collaboration, ATLAS ISDI fostered a high-energy environment where complex multi-agentic systems were built with ease.

The event established a reusable blueprint for ATLAS ISDI to continue acting as a hub for developers who are ready to move beyond the demo stage and deliver high-value, AI-powered applications to the enterprise.
