How it works

You don't attend MAL.
You build through it.

Speakers teach the decision, not the feature. Each architect builds their own app and levels it up every lab — learning the gaps, defending the trade-offs, shipping real code.

Everyone builds the same brief:
a hyperlocal marketplace with offline-first AI.
Offline-first sync On-device AI Deep links Observability Scale
🧱

Deep, then broad

Go deep in your primary stack, fluent across the others — exactly the T-shape the market hires for.

🏛️

Decisions, not just code

Every lab you write an Architecture Decision Record. Reasoning is the artifact that separates an architect from a coder.

👥

Pods + mentors

You build individually but review in pods of 4–5 with a mentor architect — never stuck, never coasting.
