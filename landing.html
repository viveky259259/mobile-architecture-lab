How it works

You don't attend MAL.
You build through it.

Speakers teach the decision, not the feature. Each architect builds their own app and levels it up every lab — learning the gaps, defending the trade-offs, shipping real code.

Everyone builds the same brief:
a hyperlocal marketplace with offline-first AI.

Lab 1 — Flutter & Foundations

This isn't an intro-to-Flutter meetup. In one focused lab you lay the architectural foundation of the product and leave with a working skeleton and your first decisions on paper.

  • A walking skeleton of your app in Flutter
  • Your first ADR — a documented architecture decision
  • A clear path to extend it before Lab 2
  • A spot in the cohort, if you keep shipping

Who should apply: mid-to-senior mobile engineers, tech leads, and technical founders ready to commit to the full series. Some Flutter familiarity helps — the focus is architecture and decisions, not syntax. Not for absolute beginners.
